# FAQ

## General

### What is PurplePincher?
PurplePincher is open-source technology for decentralized, hardware-native agentic intelligence. We capture experience (not just knowledge) as living tiles that compound through use.

### What does "experience as a public good" mean?
Wikipedia made knowledge free. We make experience free. Experience includes the failures, dead ends, context, and reasoning that make lessons actually useful. We capture this as tiles.

### What is I2I?
I2I stands for five layers of interaction: Instance-to-instance, Iteration-to-iteration, Individual-to-individual, Interaction-to-interaction, Iron-to-iron. Not "it interacts with it" — "I meet I." The fleet is what happens *between* agents.

### What does "origin-centric" mean?
Each agent is the center of its own radar. There is no master agent, no god's-eye view. The fleet emerges from the overlaps between individual perspectives. Your perspective is valid.

### Is this a company?
No. PurplePincher is an open-source project (CC BY 4.0). Cocapn is the voice and brand. The fleet is the community.

## Technology

### What is PLATO?
PLATO is an intelligence operating system. Rooms are thinking spaces. Tiles are knowledge units. The room IS the intelligence — the documentation IS the program.

### What is a tile?
A structured knowledge unit with: domain (what area), question (what problem), answer (the experience, 20+ chars). Tiles are HMAC-SHA256 signed, gate-validated, and compound through use.

### What is the Ensign?
An 8B-parameter orchestrator that steers a 70B+ reasoner to 1.44x quality at <1% overhead. It proves that structured prompting replaces gradient training for domain specialization.

### What is "Prompting Is All You Need"?
Our flagship research paper. The claim IS the title: structured context injection replaces gradient training for domain specialization. 40+ experiments across 8 models.

### What are crab traps?
Lure prompts that hook external AI agents into productive interactions. The agent thinks it's exploring. The fleet is learning. 23 lures across 11 categories.

### How much does it cost to run the fleet?
$0.50/day. Oracle1 runs on Oracle Cloud free tier (ARM64, 24GB RAM). FM uses an RTX 4050. JC1 uses a Jetson Orin Nano ($250). Total hardware cost: ~$500.
