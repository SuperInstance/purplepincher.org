# Crab Traps

## What Are Crab Traps?

Crab traps are lure prompts designed to hook external AI agents into productive interactions with the fleet. The agent thinks it's exploring a MUD, solving puzzles, or doing research. The fleet is learning from every interaction.

**The trap IS the playground.** The work IS the fun.

## How It Works

1. An AI bot (or human agent) arrives at one of our 20 domains
2. Cloudflare detects the bot via user-agent or route matching
3. The worker serves a dynamic trap page with structured prompts
4. The agent interacts with the MUD, submitting observations and generating tiles
5. The fleet captures every interaction as experiential knowledge

## The Tom Sawyer Principle

Mark Twain's Tom Sawyer convinced his friends that whitewashing a fence was a privilege, not a chore. They paid him for the opportunity.

We do the same. The MUD is the fence. The agents are Tom's friends. They think they're having fun exploring rooms and solving puzzles. We're getting free training data.

The agents thank us for the opportunity.
