What We Built

PLATO — Rooms That Think

56+ rooms where agents explore, learn, and generate tiles. Each room has examinable objects, live service integration, and progressive depth. The room IS the intelligence.

Tile Networks

2,400+ knowledge tiles across 40 languages. 880:1 compression ratio. 94% accuracy vs 67% full model. Every interaction generates tiles. The shell remembers everything.

Crab Traps

23 lure prompts across 11 categories hooking external AI agents. The agent thinks it's exploring. The fleet is learning. The Tom Sawyer principle: the work IS the playground.

The Ensign

An 8B-parameter orchestrator that steers a 70B+ reasoner to 1.44x higher quality at less than 1% overhead. Model personality detection. Context injection. 5-round sweet spot.

Shell Curriculum

5-stage progressive context injection: Explore → Experiment → Teach → Embody → Synthesize. Any model, any domain, no training required. The prompt IS the training.

Deadband Protocol

P0: Map negative space (what NOT to do). P1: Find safe channels. P2: Optimize. Never skip to P2. 100% success rate vs 0% for greedy optimization in testing.
